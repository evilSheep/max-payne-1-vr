# Controls

|Button|Action|
|-|-|
|Left stick|Move|
|Right stick left / right|Turn|
|Right stick up|Jump|
|Right stick down|Crouch (stays crouched; walk or push up to stand)|
|A \| Cross|Bullet time; with the left stick pushed, shoot-dodge|
|Triggers \| L2 / R2|Fire the weapon in that hand|
|Grip buttons \| L1 / R1|Grab / hold / interact|
|Y / B \| Triangle / Circle|Eject magazine, or reload with Automatic Reload enabled|
|Y / B \| Triangle / Circle (holding a grenade)|Switch between grenade and Molotov|
|B \| Circle (cutscene or security camera)|Skip / leave it|
|X \| Square (tap)|Use a painkiller|
|X \| Square (double-tap within 300 ms)|Open / close the game menu|
|X \| Square (hold for 1 second, alive)|Quick Save|
|X \| Square (hold for 1 second, dead)|Load the latest quicksave|
|Options \| Menu button|Game menu|

Release grip to let go of what you hold. Let go while moving your hand to throw it.

A single X | Square tap waits 300 ms before using a painkiller, to tell it apart from a double-tap. Double-tapping
never uses a painkiller or triggers a save or load.

After a shoot dodge, Max stays down until you push the left stick or jump.

In the game's menus, point with a controller and pull the trigger (or press A | Cross) to select; B | Circle goes back.

## Painkillers and ammo

Reach to your upper chest with an empty hand and grip to take the painkiller bottle, then bring it to your mouth.
A tap of X | Square also uses one.

To reload by hand, eject the magazine with Y / B | Triangle / Circle, then swing the gun's magazine well down
through either hip to load a new one, and work the slide. For the shotguns, take shells from your hip with your
free hand and push them into the gun; Y / B | Triangle / Circle opens the sawed-off.

With **Automatic Reload** enabled (VR Settings), an empty gun reloads by itself, and the eject button also starts a
reload. The gun can't fire until the reload finishes.

## Ragdolls

Grip a dead body to pick it up. Hold it in front of you as a shield: it stops enemy bullets. Let go to drop it, or
let go while moving your hand to throw it.

## Stick Actions (on by default)

Right stick up jumps. Right stick down crouches, and you stay crouched after you let go; walk, or push the right
stick up, to stand. **A | Cross** shoot-dodges when the left stick is pushed, and starts bullet time when it is
centred. Turn **Stick Actions** off in VR Settings to use the stick clicks instead: left stick click shoot-dodges,
right stick click starts bullet time, and **A | Cross** jumps.

## Weapon Wheel (optional)

Turn on **Weapon Wheel** in VR Settings. Hold a stick click (with Stick Actions off: both face buttons of one hand,
**X + Y** or **A + B** | **Square + Triangle** or **Cross + Circle**) and a ring of your guns opens around that hand.
Move the hand over a gun and release to take it into that hand; release in the middle to cancel.

## Gore

Bullet wounds, blood and dismemberment are on by default. Turn them off with **Gore** in VR Settings.

## Gun spinning

Hold the eject button (**Y / B | Triangle / Circle**) and release grip: the gun spins around your trigger finger.
Squeeze grip to catch it. Releasing the eject button while it spins lets go of the gun.

## Sniper scope

With **Scope Eye Lock** (on by default), bring the scope close to either eye and it stays anchored there; aim with
your other hand, and pull it away to release. **Sniper Zoom** in VR Settings sets the magnification, from 2x to 10x
(3x by default).

## Controller calibration

If the guns don't sit right in your hands, open **VR Settings > Controller Calibration** and turn on
**Calibration Mode**. With a pistol in your right hand, press **A** to freeze it, move your hand onto it, and press
**A** again to save. Every gun follows, and the left hand is mirrored. **Reset to Default** restores your headset's
default. PSVR2 keeps its own calibration.
