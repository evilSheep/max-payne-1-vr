4K textures - "25th Anniversary Remaster" by andrew22
=====================================================

The MaxPayneVR-4K-Textures-part*.zip downloads contain the "25th Anniversary Remaster"
texture pack by andrew22, a complete 4x remaster of the game's textures:
  https://www.nexusmods.com/maxpayne/mods/45

They are included in this release with andrew22's permission. All credit for the
textures goes to andrew22. They remain his work; this permission covers their use
with Max Payne VR only. Please don't extract or redistribute them separately - get
the pack itself from the Nexus Mods page above.

Left out of this release: the pack's weapon sounds and its globalconfig.txt.

The installer keeps the textures in the game's mp1vr_hd folder. They are on by
default; Options > VR Settings > 4K Textures switches them off (restart the game).
